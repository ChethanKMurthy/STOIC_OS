# STOIC OS — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 May 2026_

STOIC OS is a personal macOS application that runs on your own device. This
policy describes how it handles your data.

## Summary

STOIC OS is local-first. Your personal data — decisions, goals, reflections,
time logs, and any connected health data — is stored on your own Mac. STOIC OS
does not operate any server that collects, stores, or receives your personal
data. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

## Data the app handles

- **Information you enter** — decisions, goals, your defined ideal self,
  time-audit entries, and similar notes. Stored locally on your device.
- **Health and fitness data (WHOOP)** — if you connect your WHOOP account, the
  app retrieves recovery, sleep, strain, and workout data from the WHOOP API
  using access you explicitly authorize. It is used only within the app, on
  your device, to inform planning and reasoning. It is stored locally and is
  never sold or shared.
- **Calendar data** — if you connect Google or Microsoft calendars, the app
  reads (and, if you allow it, writes) calendar events to plan your time. This
  data stays on your device.

## Where your data goes

- **On-device processing** — the app's reasoning runs on a local model on your
  Mac. The content of your decisions and reflections is not sent to any external
  service for processing.
- **Network use is limited to:**
  - a one-time download of the local model,
  - requests to WHOOP, Google, or Microsoft APIs that you explicitly connect
    and authorize.
- No other data leaves your device. No telemetry. No analytics.

## Third-party services

When you connect them, the app communicates directly with:

- **WHOOP** — governed by WHOOP's own privacy policy.
- **Google / Microsoft** — governed by their respective privacy policies.

The app requests only the access scopes needed for the features you use, and you
can revoke that access at any time from the provider's account settings.

## Storage and security

Your data — including access tokens for connected services such as WHOOP — is
stored in the application's local data folder on your Mac.

## Your control

- You can delete your local data at any time.
- You can disconnect WHOOP, Google, or Microsoft at any time, and revoke the
  app's access from those providers' account settings.
